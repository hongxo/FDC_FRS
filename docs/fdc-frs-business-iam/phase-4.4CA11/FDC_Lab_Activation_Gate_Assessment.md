# Gate assessment

| Gate | Status | Missing evidence |
|---|---|---|
| G1 Technical complete | BLOCKED | End-to-end revocation policy, worker crash, BFCache/logout, rebind |
| G2 Isolated acceptance | BLOCKED | Mandatory scenarios remain open, multi-node and restore not attested |
| G3 Independent acceptance | PENDING | Reviewer assignment, exact SHA review, findings, signed disposition |
| G4 Lab activation | PENDING / unauthorized | Policy dispositions, OT scope, permit, approved SHA/change/rollback and separate authorization |
| G5 Production | NO-GO | Outside A.11 scope |

Implemented, tested and isolated evidence apply only to individual components. Independently reviewed, policy approved, Lab authorized and Lab activated are separate states.
